A No-Nonsense Guide for Men & Adults: How to Get Sorted at the GP Without the Hassle

4 min read · The Kaur Care Journal

Plenty of us put things off because booking feels like more hassle than the problem. Here is the no-nonsense version of getting sorted, with the least faff.

Start at the pharmacy for the common stuff

You can get seen the same day, free, for a sore throat, sinusitis, shingles, infected bites and more under NHS Pharmacy First, no GP appointment and no referral. For adults, newer pharmacy pathways for things like outer ear infection are also being rolled out in many areas; it is worth asking.

Use the online front door

Most practices now take online requests through the NHS App, eConsult or Patchs. A clear written request often gets triaged the same day, no 8 AM phone queue. Say what you need, what is happening and since when, what you have tried, and how it is affecting you.

Do not sit on the things that matter

Some symptoms are worth a prompt appointment rather than a wait: a new or changing lump, blood where it should not be, unexplained weight loss, a mole that is changing, chest pain on exertion, or problems passing urine. You do not need to self-diagnose; you just need to get it looked at.

Make the appointment count

Lead with the real reason you came, even if it is awkward. Note your main symptom, how long, and any questions in advance. Before you leave, confirm the plan and when to follow up. For anything severe or sudden, chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke, call 999.

Walk in prepared

Build a clear 1-page GP brief or a Right to Choose referral letter in a couple of minutes, then take it to your appointment. Free, and your details stay on your device.
