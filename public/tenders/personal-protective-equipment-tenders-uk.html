What personal protective equipment (PPE) tenders cover

PPE procurement in the UK spans a broad range of contracts, most commonly:

  • gloves, gowns and aprons
  • surgical and respirator masks
  • eye protection and face shields
  • clinical waste and decontamination supplies

Who buys personal protective equipment (PPE) in the UK

The main buyers are NHS trusts, integrated care boards (ICBs), NHS Supply Chain and local authorities. Understanding which body owns the budget, and whether they buy through a framework, a Dynamic Purchasing System or open tender, is often the difference between a bid that lands and one that never gets seen.

How to win personal protective equipment (PPE) contracts

  • Evidence standards compliance (EN/ASTM) and independent test certificates
  • Demonstrate stock holding and lead-time resilience post-pandemic
  • Offer transparent, benchmarkable pricing
