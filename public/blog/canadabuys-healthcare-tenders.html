CanadaBuys Healthcare Tenders: A Supplier's Guide

CanadaBuys is the Government of Canada's official procurement service, and it publishes open tender notices — including a steady stream of healthcare and medical opportunities — as machine-readable open data. For suppliers already selling into the US or UK, Canada is an accessible next market.

How Canadian public procurement is structured

  • Federal — CanadaBuys is the single window for federal opportunities (replacing the older Buyandsell/MERX GETS route)
  • Provincial & territorial — each province runs its own portal; health authorities buy at this level too
  • Group purchasing organisations — much clinical purchasing runs through regional health-authority GPOs

Finding healthcare opportunities on CanadaBuys

CanadaBuys classifies notices with UNSPSC codes rather than CPV. Medical and health opportunities cluster in the medical-equipment, pharmaceutical and health-services segments. Healthcare buyers include federal bodies (e.g. Indigenous Services, the military health system, Veterans Affairs) plus, at provincial level, the health authorities.

Tips for bidding into Canada

  • Watch for trade-agreement thresholds (CFTA/CETA) that open contracts to more bidders
  • Bilingual (English/French) documentation is often required
  • Register early — some processes need supplier registration before you can respond
  • Benchmark against award notices — see how to read a contract award notice

Frequently asked questions

What classification codes does CanadaBuys use?

UNSPSC codes. Healthcare opportunities appear mainly in the medical equipment, pharmaceutical and health-services segments.

Did CanadaBuys replace Buyandsell.gc.ca?

Yes — CanadaBuys is the current single window for Government of Canada tender opportunities.

Can non-Canadian suppliers bid?

Often yes, particularly where trade agreements (CFTA, CETA, CPTPP) apply and open the contract to international competition.

Track Canadian healthcare tenders

CanadaBuys healthcare opportunities alongside the EU, UK, US and global feeds. Free tier, no credit card.
