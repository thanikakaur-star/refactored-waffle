10 Reasons You're Losing Healthcare Tenders (and How to Fix Them)

Most lost healthcare tenders aren't lost on product quality — they're lost on process: finding opportunities too late, missing a mandatory requirement, pricing blind, or submitting a generic response. Here are the 10 most common reasons suppliers lose, and the practical fix for each.

1. You found the tender too late

The problem: healthcare tenders are scattered across TED Europa, SAM.gov, WHO and NHS portals, each with tight deadlines. The fix: monitor all your relevant sources daily, or use a tool that aggregates them — see tender alerts.

2. You missed a mandatory requirement

The problem: one missing certification or document and you're disqualified, however strong the rest of your bid. The fix: build a compliance checklist from the tender documents and verify every item before submission — covered in responding to an RFP.

3. You priced blind

The problem: pricing on cost-plus alone, with no idea what the market actually pays. The fix: benchmark against real contract award data before setting your number.

4. Your bid was generic

The problem: boilerplate answers that don't address the specific evaluation criteria score poorly, however well-written. The fix: structure your response around the buyer's exact published criteria, in order, with evidence for each.

5. You didn't do a proper bid/no-bid assessment

The problem: chasing every tender, including ones you can't realistically win, wastes effort that could go into stronger bids. The fix: be disciplined about fit before committing resources.

6. Your social value case was an afterthought

The problem: vague, generic social value statements score badly against buyers who now formally weight this criterion. The fix: build a real, evidenced case — see social value in healthcare procurement.

7. You missed a framework renewal window

The problem: missing the tender for a framework you needed means waiting years for the next opportunity. The fix: track renewal dates for frameworks in your category — see framework agreements explained.

8. You underestimated compliance requirements

The problem: discovering late that you're missing a required certification (ISO 13485, UKCA, WHO prequalification) kills a bid you'd otherwise win. The fix: map compliance requirements for your category well before a relevant tender appears.

9. You didn't evidence supply chain resilience

The problem: buyers increasingly score supply security, and a single-source, single-country supply chain looks risky. The fix: be ready to demonstrate diversified sourcing and contingency plans.

10. You submitted at the last minute

The problem: portal errors, file size limits and last-minute reviews eat time — and a late submission is often simply rejected. The fix: build in a buffer and submit at least a day early.
