Privacy, in plain English

Your data, and how we look after it

Kaur Care is a care-navigation tool. We built it so that the most sensitive thing you do here, writing down your symptoms, never leaves your own device.

The short version. The Right to Choose referral letter and the 1-page GP brief are generated entirely in your browser. The health details you type into them are never sent to us, never stored by us, and never seen by us. We only hold a small record when you buy an unlock, so you can get back into the tools.

Who we are

Kaur Care is the data controller for any personal data described here. We handle data in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can reach us at privacy@kaurcare.com.

The generators run on your device

When you create a GP brief or a Right to Choose letter, everything you type, your name, date of birth, symptoms, cycle details, ADHD or autism notes, the lot, stays in your browser. We do not transmit it, log it or store it. When you copy, print or download the document, that happens locally too. If you close the tab without saving the file, it is gone.

What we do collect, and why

When you unlock the prep tools. If you buy the £4.99 Fast-Track Pass or the £7.99 Family & Women's Health Pass, we store your email address, your postcode, the general condition keyword you searched, the plan type and whether the unlock is active. We use this to give you what you paid for and to let you re-open the tools later. Your card details are handled directly by Stripe, our payment processor, and never touch our servers.

Partner links. If you arrive through an employer or partner link, we store that partner's reference alongside an unlock, so sponsored access works and any revenue share can be accounted for. No health details are attached.

Basic technical logs. Like any website, our servers keep short-lived operational logs (for example, to keep the service secure and working). These do not contain the contents of your documents.

Our lawful bases

We rely on performance of a contract to provide an unlock you have purchased, and on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the service. Where a law requires consent, we ask for it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use a small number of trusted processors to run the service: Stripe for payments, Supabase for database hosting, and Resend for any email we send you. Each only processes what is needed for its job.

How long we keep it

Unlock records are kept for as long as you may want to re-access the tools and for as long as we must keep them for tax and accounting, then deleted. You can ask us to delete your record sooner (see your rights below).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable form. Email privacy@kaurcare.com and we will respond within the statutory timeframe. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and browser storage

We use a little local browser storage for conveniences such as remembering an unlock on your device. We do not use advertising trackers.

A reminder

Kaur Care is a navigation and directory tool, not medical advice, and it does not diagnose or prescribe. Always confirm eligibility with your pharmacy, GP or NHS 111, and for anything serious call 999 or 111.
