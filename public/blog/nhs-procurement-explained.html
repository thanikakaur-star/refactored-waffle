NHS Procurement Explained: How the NHS Buys

The NHS is one of the largest buyers in the world, spending tens of billions a year on everything from medicines and devices to clinical staffing and IT. For suppliers, understanding how the NHS buys is the difference between winning work and watching contracts go to competitors who understood the system.

Who actually does the buying

  • NHS trusts and foundation trusts, which run their own procurement for many goods and services
  • Integrated care boards (ICBs), which commission services at population scale, see selling to ICBs
  • NHS Supply Chain (SCCL), which runs national framework agreements for common products
  • Local authorities, for social-care-adjacent and community services

The routes to market

The NHS almost never buys by simply picking a supplier. Instead it uses recognised procurement routes:

  • Frameworks, pre-approved supplier lists that trusts call off from, see frameworks explained
  • Dynamic Purchasing Systems (DPS), open lists you can join at any time, see framework vs DPS
  • Open tenders for one-off or higher-value requirements
  • Direct award below certain thresholds, see procurement thresholds

Where NHS tenders are published

Above-threshold contracts appear on Find a Tender, below-threshold ones on Contracts Finder, and framework notices via NHS Supply Chain. Because opportunities are spread across all three (plus dozens of trust portals), suppliers increasingly monitor them in one place rather than checking each by hand, see how to find NHS tenders and browse live ones on our tenders hub.

How suppliers win NHS work

  • Register on the right frameworks and DPS for your category early
  • Lead bids with quality, workforce and social value, not just price, see bid-writing tips
  • Use award data to benchmark price and see who is winning
  • Get in early, see becoming an NHS supplier

Frequently asked questions

How does NHS procurement work?

The NHS buys through formal routes, frameworks, Dynamic Purchasing Systems, open tenders and (below thresholds) direct award, publishing opportunities on Find a Tender, Contracts Finder and NHS Supply Chain.

How do I find NHS tenders and contracts?

Monitor Find a Tender and Contracts Finder plus NHS Supply Chain notices. Aggregators pull all of these into one searchable feed with alerts.

Do I have to be on a framework to sell to the NHS?

Not always, but many purchases go through frameworks or DPS, so being listed on the right ones significantly widens your access.
