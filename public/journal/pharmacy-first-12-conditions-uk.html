12 Things You Can Get Sorted at a UK Pharmacy Today (Without the 8 AM GP Queue)

4 min read · The Kaur Care Journal

You wake up, something hurts, and the only plan seems to be the 8 AM phone scramble. For a growing list of everyday conditions, there is a faster route: walk into a pharmacy and be seen the same day, free, no appointment and no GP referral.

This is NHS Pharmacy First in England. Since early 2024 your community pharmacist has been able to assess, and where appropriate supply treatment including some prescription-only medicines, for a defined set of conditions. It is quick, private, and takes real pressure off the GP queue.

The seven available right now

These are nationwide. Age and eligibility rules apply, and the pharmacist will check them with you.

  • UTIs in women aged 16 to 64 with uncomplicated symptoms.
  • Sore throat for anyone aged 5 and over.
  • Earache in children aged 1 to 17 (this one must be in person).
  • Sinusitis for anyone aged 12 and over.
  • Impetigo for anyone aged 1 and over.
  • Infected insect bites for anyone aged 1 and over.
  • Shingles for adults aged 18 and over (get seen early).

Five more being rolled out

Through late 2026, NHS pharmacy services have been expanding. Depending on your area and pharmacy, you may also be able to get help with adult outer ear infection, children's hay fever, mild to moderate acne, episodic migraine in adults, and mild skin and soft-tissue infections or scabies.

These newer pathways are still being introduced and availability varies, so treat them as "worth asking about" rather than guaranteed. A quick call to your local pharmacy, or a check of current NHS England guidance, confirms what is on offer near you.

When the pharmacy will send you onward

Pharmacists are trained to spot when something needs more. Trouble breathing or swallowing, a very high fever, a stiff neck, spreading infection, or simply feeling very unwell are jobs for 111 or 999, not a pharmacy counter. Trust that steer.
