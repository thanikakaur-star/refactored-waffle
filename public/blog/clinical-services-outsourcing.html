Clinical Services Outsourcing: Tenders & Frameworks

Clinical services outsourcing covers the growing market for delivering clinical care under contract, from teleradiology reporting and elective insourcing to community services and clinical staffing. Waiting-list recovery has expanded this category sharply.

Common clinical service contracts

  • Diagnostics reporting, outsourced teleradiology and pathology to clear backlogs
  • Elective insourcing / outsourcing, additional capacity for planned procedures
  • Community services, nursing, therapy and long-term condition management
  • Clinical staffing frameworks, locum and agency provision

How these are evaluated

Clinical governance is front and centre. Buyers scrutinise CQC registration and ratings, clinical outcomes, workforce credentialing and information governance far more than in a product tender. Price still matters, but a weak clinical-quality response is usually fatal.

Finding clinical service tenders

These appear on Find a Tender and Contracts Finder (and TED in the EU), commissioned by trusts and integrated care boards. The allied health slice, physiotherapy, OT, is a related but distinct category; see allied health procurement.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between insourcing and outsourcing?

Insourcing brings an external clinical team to work within the hospital's own facilities; outsourcing sends the work to the provider's site. Both are procured via tender.

What matters most in a clinical services bid?

Clinical governance, CQC registration/ratings, outcomes, workforce credentialing and information governance, typically outweighs price.
