10 Bid-Writing Tips for Healthcare Tenders

Most healthcare tenders are won or lost on the quality responses, not the price. These ten tips come straight from what evaluators reward — and penalise — on NHS and public-sector bids.

The ten tips

  • Answer the question asked — score against the published criteria, not the story you want to tell
  • Evidence everything — replace claims with numbers, case studies and named outcomes
  • Mirror the weighting — put your effort where the marks are
  • Lead with outcomes — waiting times, clinical results, patient experience
  • Show workforce resilience — recruitment, retention, training and cover
  • Build in social value deliberately — see sustainable procurement
  • Price with evidence — benchmark against award data, see pricing a bid
  • Make it easy to mark — headings that match the question, no wall of text
  • Get the compliance basics right — a failed pass/fail question sinks the whole bid
  • Start early — a good bid team needs runway, see building a bid team

The mistakes that lose bids

Generic boilerplate, ignoring the weighting, unevidenced claims and missing a mandatory requirement are the recurring reasons good providers still lose. For a fuller list see common mistakes that lose tenders.

Frequently asked questions

What matters more in healthcare tenders, price or quality?

Usually quality — clinical outcomes, governance and social value typically carry the majority of the marks, with price a smaller share.

How early should you start a bid?

As early as possible — the strongest bids are planned before the tender even drops, using pipeline intelligence to anticipate it.
